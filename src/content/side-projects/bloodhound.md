Bloodhound was designed to compete in the 2017 FIRST Robotics Competition by Team 973, and won the 2017 FRC World Championship in Houston, Texas. As a mentor on Team 973 I coordinated manufacturing and installation of improved mechanisms onto our alliance partners' robots to give them a competitive edge.
